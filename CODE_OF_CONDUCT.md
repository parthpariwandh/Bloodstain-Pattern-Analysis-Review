# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to foster an open, welcoming, and respectful environment for scientific collaboration.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Respectful and constructive technical feedback
- Evidence-based scientific discussion
- Proper citation and attribution

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, and plagiarism.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take action in response to inappropriate behavior.
